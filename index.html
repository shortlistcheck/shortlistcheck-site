The problem

AI assistants are now your buyers’ first stop.

Buyers ask AI for recommendations before they reach out to sales.

If you are not shortlisted, competitors get compared instead.

You need public signals that AI can see, explain, and trust.

Audit method

A monthly readout built around buying behavior.

Generic mention trackers can make a brand look visible while competitors still win the recommendation. ShortlistCheck focuses on whether your product is shortlisted when a buyer is actually evaluating options.
